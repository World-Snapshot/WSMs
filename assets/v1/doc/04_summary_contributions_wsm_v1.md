Apart from <a style="color: green;">**trying to solve the truly difficult challenges**</a>, our current contributions can be summarized as follows:

- We propose the <a style="color: green;">**Unified Law for Visual Tasks**</a> and its four executable visual interfaces.
- We introduce <a style="color: green;">**World Snapshot Graphics and World Snapshot Models**</a>, turning rich world information into generatable image-like targets.
- We propose <a style="color: green;">**TunnelingNet**</a>, enabling thousands of task routes to share one generator while remaining independently trainable and improvable.
- We validate <a style="color: green;">**1,148 visual tasks in one generative system**</a>, configure 3,052 tasks, and release qualitative, quantitative, and interface-level evidence for the task bank.
